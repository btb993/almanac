# Daily Almanac

A personal daily app to read instead of scrolling a feed.

- **Today**: a fact, a classic poem, a story from history, a word of the day, "on this day", an artwork, NASA's astronomy picture and a Wikipedia long read.
- **News**: *The Daily Mind*, your own front page on the topics you choose (The Guardian, Hacker News, Wikipedia).
- **Sky**: your birth chart, a reading of each planet, what affects you today and the transits coming up. Planet positions are calculated on the device.
- **Releases**: new music from artists you follow (MusicBrainz), books from your authors (Open Library), game releases (RAWG, optional key) and reviews.
- **Saved**: a corkboard of everything you bookmark.

Settings live in the browser on each device. Use "Copy my setup" / "Paste a setup" to move them between devices.

Installs as an app from the browser menu ("Add to Home screen" / "Install app").
